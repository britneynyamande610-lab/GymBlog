4-Day Upper/Lower Hypertrophy Plan

Evidence-aware, simple structure. Warm up with 5-10 minutes light cardio and ramp-up sets.

Weekly Layout

  • Mon - Upper
  • Tue - Lower
  • Thu - Upper
  • Sat - Lower

Weekly schedule

Weekly training schedule
Day Focus Notes
Day 1 Upper A Push emphasis
Day 2 Lower A Quad emphasis
Day 3 Rest or active recovery Light cardio, mobility
Day 4 Upper B Pull emphasis
Day 5 Lower B Glute/ham emphasis
Day 6-7 Rest Sleep, steps, stretch

Session Templates

Upper A

  • Bench Press - 3x6-8 (2 RIR)
  • Chin-Up - 3x6-8 (2 RIR)
  • Incline DB Press - 3x8-10
  • One-Arm Row - 3x8-10
  • Lateral Raise - 3x12-15
  • Cable Curl - 2x10-12
  • Rope Pressdown - 2x10-12

Lower A

  • Back Squat - 3x5-8
  • Romanian Deadlift - 3x6-8
  • Leg Press - 3x10-12
  • Leg Curl - 3x10-12
  • Calf Raise - 4x8-12
  • Hanging Knee Raise - 3x10-15

Upper B

  • Overhead Press - 3x6-8
  • Chest-Supported Row - 3x8-10
  • Dips (assisted if needed) - 3x8-12
  • Lat Pulldown - 3x8-12
  • Rear Delt Fly - 3x12-15
  • Incline Curl - 2x10-12
  • Overhead Triceps Ext - 2x10-12

Lower B

  • Deadlift (or Trap Bar) - 3x3-5
  • Front Squat - 3x6-8
  • Split Squat - 3x8-10/leg
  • Leg Curl - 3x10-12
  • Seated Calf Raise - 4x10-15
  • Plank - 3x:45-60

Progression & RPE

Work mostly at RPE 7-9 (1-3 RIR). Add reps first, then load. Keep a logbook.
