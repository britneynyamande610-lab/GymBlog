Quick start

What you need to know to start growing today.

Volume

  • Sets/week: 10-20 per muscle
  • Sessions: Train each muscle 2x/week
  • Structure: Mix compound and isolation lifts

Intensity & effort

  • Reps: Core work in 6-12 range
  • Load: ~65-85% of 1RM
  • RIR: Stop 0-3 reps before failure

Progression

  • Add: Reps → load → sets (in that order)
  • Rest: 2-3 min compounds, 1-2 min isolation
  • Track: Logbook every session

Technique

  • Range: Full ROM you can control
  • Eccentric: Control the lowering
  • Stability: Reduce momentum

Nutrition

  • Protein: 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day
  • Calories: 5-15% surplus for growth
  • Carbs: Fuel training performance

Recovery

  • Sleep: 7-9 hours
  • Deload: Every 6-10 weeks as needed
  • Stress: Keep daily stressors in check

Why this works

Hypertrophy thrives on adequate volume, proximity to failure, and progressive overload — supported by good sleep and nutrition. This site distills the big rocks so you can spend more time lifting and less time guessing.
